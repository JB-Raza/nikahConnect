# 1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW

The application is a Muslim-focused matrimonial and social platform that enables users to:

* Discover potential marriage partners
* Communicate with matched users
* Build social connections
* Participate in community discussions
* Join groups and communities
* Share content through a social feed

The application contains both Matrimony and Social Networking modules.

# manual checking:
## initial workflow:
    1. app starts, asks for registration using email or google.
    2. takes information from the account, and asks further personalized questions about personality, likes, dislikes, etc.
    3. asks for our interests, preferences, height, marriage status, children, education, profesion, age, marriage plan, search by location (country wise, city wise) etc.
    4. asks for person's marriage intentions (how he plans to persue i.e: first month only chatting, next month, parent's meetup, wanna marry within 4 to 12 months etc)
    5. filters:
        - free users can have upto 4 filters, premium and gold users can have 21 filters.
        - free filters are age, location, sect and ethnicity

## Tabs screens:
    -total 5 screens:
        1.marriage screen: suggests a proposal profile, you have full information about that person (mentioned in profile), including photos, videos, interests etc added.
            -you can like a person or pass it. or send a compliment message (you get limited compliment messages (2 only, then you have to upgrade))
            -you can mark someone farourite, report or block
            -once you like a person, he is added in pending chats, once both parties like each other, they can start chatting.
            -sort people by your filters (premium only)
        2. Explore screen: has 3 sections: 1st shows currently available, 2nd shows newly joined and 3rd shows active near you

        3. Jamaa Screen:
            -has a top filter tab horizontally scrollable.
            -shows general feed depending upon your interests, and active filter
            -like comment and share post features.
            -you can also write your own posts (just like facebook flow)
            -comments are following tree structure ( a comment can be replied which further can also be replied making a chain like in twitter)
        4. chat Screen:
            -has 2 tabs: 1 is marriage tab and other is friends tab
            -marriage tab contains the proposal where both parties mutually liked when you both have sent each other a message, only 5 chats allowed at a time, unlock with gold or premium.
            -friends tab includes general friends you added from Jamaa Screen.
            -archive a chat
        5. user Profile:
            - has 2 tabs: Marriage, social
            - marriage profile that has name, photo and other marriage related information we added.
            - has a get more likes and more compliments by subscribing to gold plan.
            -edit profile, search filters, settings
            -social profile tab:
                -edit profile, saved posts, settings, help & support, invite friends
                -setup your profile, you can join groups, set username

Other screens:
    -notification screen (on top of the header on most screens)

other functionalities:
    - we can click the boost icon on top header to boost our profile (profile will get more recommendations for the boost time. 3h free then has to get gold plan to get weekly boosts)
